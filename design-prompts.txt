KAZI MOBILE — SCREEN DESIGN PROMPTS
=====================================
For use with Claude Design (or Stitch). Paste the FOUNDATION prompt first in a
new canvas/project to lock in the visual language, then paste each screen
prompt as a follow-up message in that SAME canvas, one at a time, so every
screen stays visually consistent.

Suggested mobile nav: bottom tabs for Dashboard / Tasks / Inventory / Finance
/ More, with everything else reachable from "More".


=====================================
0 — FOUNDATION (run first)
=====================================
Design the visual foundation for "Kazi", a mobile ERP app for a garment
manufacturing company operating out of Nepal and the UK. This is an internal
ops tool used by factory staff, admins, and directors on their phones — not a
consumer app. It needs to feel calm, precise, and trustworthy, like a
well-run factory floor: lots of dense data (numbers, statuses, tables) shown
without feeling cluttered.

Color direction: take inspiration from a deep forest-green / mint brand
identity — a dark ink-green for primary text and a dark sidebar/nav, a soft
warm off-white paper background, mint-green as the primary accent for
positive states and CTAs, and a warm terracotta/clay tone for alerts or
destructive actions. Don't copy this literally — use it as a starting
palette and refine it into something that feels native and polished on
mobile, including a coherent dark mode.

Typography: clean, modern grotesk sans-serif, tabular numerals for
data-heavy screens. Favor card-based layouts with soft shadows and rounded
corners over flat lists. Establish reusable primitives: stat/KPI cards with
small trend indicators, status pills (color-coded by state), avatars with
initials, buttons (primary/ghost/danger/secondary), and a bottom tab bar
with 5 slots (Dashboard, Tasks, Inventory, Finance, More).

Generate a style guide screen showing these primitives before we move to
individual screens.


=====================================
1 — LOGIN
=====================================
Design the login screen for the Kazi mobile ERP app, following the
established style guide. This is a secure portal for factory and office
staff — Nepal and UK teams.

Include: centered logo/wordmark, a short tagline ("Secure portal for Nepal
and UK operations teams"), email field, password field with a "Forgot
password?" link, a primary "Sign In" button, and a loading state on submit.
Also design the "forgot password" state: email field, "Send Reset Link"
button, and a confirmation state ("Check your inbox") with a back-to-login
link. Keep it minimal — this is a utility screen, not a marketing one.


=====================================
2 — DASHBOARD
=====================================
Design the Dashboard home screen — the first thing staff see after login,
following the established style guide.

Include: a greeting header (name + role), a row of KPI/stat cards (e.g.
Active Orders, Attendance Today, Items Below Reorder Threshold, Awaiting
Your Approval) each with a value, trend indicator, and small sparkline.
Below that, an "Active orders by stage" widget showing a pipeline breakdown
(Order Received → Fabric Sourcing → Cutting → Finishing & Pressing →
Packing → Delivered) as a horizontal segmented bar or stage list with
counts. Add a compact attendance summary (Present/Absent/Late/Leave counts)
and a "needs your approval" list of pending items with quick approve/reject
actions. Use pull-to-refresh. This is the busiest screen in the app —
prioritize scannability over density.


=====================================
3 — TASKS
=====================================
Design the Tasks screen, following the established style guide. It's a
lightweight task/kanban tool for staff, with tasks having a status (In
Progress, Done, Blocked, Inactive), an assignee (avatar), and a due date,
with an option to flag "Due today".

Design it as a mobile-friendly board: either horizontally-swipeable status
columns or a single filterable list grouped by status, with a floating
action button to add a task. Include a task detail/edit sheet (bottom
sheet) showing title, assignee picker, due date, status selector, and a
delete action.


=====================================
4 — ATTENDANCE
=====================================
Design the Attendance screen, following the established style guide. Staff
clock in/out from their phone, and admins review team attendance.

Include a prominent "Clock In / Clock Out" card at the top showing current
status and total hours today. Below it, a team attendance list/calendar view
with status pills per person per day: Present, Absent, Late, Half-day,
Leave. Include a monthly summary showing total hours and any salary cuts
tied to attendance. Support switching between "My Attendance" and "Team"
views if the user is an admin.


=====================================
5 — PRODUCTION
=====================================
Design the Production screen, following the established style guide. This
tracks manufacturing batches through a garment production pipeline (e.g.
Cutting, Fabric Sourcing, Finishing & Pressing, Packing).

Include a filterable list of active production batches, each showing
product (e.g. "Chinese Terry Fabric", "Hoodies"), current stage, and status
(Active/Cancelled/etc). Add an "Add Batch" flow, a batch detail view with a
visual stage tracker, notes/comments with photo attachments, and a
production calendar view for scheduling. Support attaching pictures to
batches (camera/upload).


=====================================
6 — QUALITY CONTROL
=====================================
Design the Quality Control screen, following the established style guide.
It's a checklist-style review tool used to inspect production batches
before they move to the next stage.

Show a list of items pending QC review, each with pass/fail/flag actions,
and a detail view for logging inspection notes and photos against a batch.
Keep it fast to use one-handed on a factory floor.


=====================================
7 — INVENTORY & LIBRARY
=====================================
Design the Inventory & Library screen, following the established style
guide. This tracks fabric and material stock (e.g. "Anti-Grunge Cotton",
"AP Cotton") with reorder thresholds, plus a "library" of reference items
(sketches, specs).

Include a searchable/filterable inventory list showing item, quantity, and
a "below reorder threshold" warning state. Add an "Add Fabric / Add Item"
flow with an image upload step ("Click or drag to upload"), and an item
detail view showing stock history. Since this screen bundles a lot of
functionality, design it with a segmented control or tabs at the top to
switch between "Inventory" and "Library".


=====================================
8 — PURCHASES
=====================================
Design the Purchases screen, following the established style guide. It's a
purchase-order log for buying materials, grouped and listing amounts,
payment method (Cash/Bank), and status.

Show a list of purchase entries grouped by supplier or date, each showing
amount, payment method, and status pill. Include a form to add a new
purchase entry with supplier, item, amount, payment method, and date.


=====================================
9 — ORDER MANAGEMENT
=====================================
Design the Order Management screen, following the established style guide.
It tracks customer orders through a fulfillment pipeline with priority
levels.

Show a list of orders with client name, product (e.g. "Hoodies"), current
stage (Order Received → Cutting → ... → Delivered), priority (Normal/High),
and status (Active/Cancelled/Delivered). Include a "Create Order" flow and
an order detail view with a stage-advance action ("Advance Stage") and a
priority-change control. Design this to feel fast for triaging many orders
at once — favor a dense list with clear status pills over big cards.


=====================================
10 — SALES
=====================================
Design the Sales screen, following the established style guide. It's a
sales overview combining a pipeline view and summary metrics.

Include stat cards for "Active Orders", "New This Week", and "Delivered
This Month", followed by an order pipeline list showing customer, date, and
current stage (Fabric Sourcing, Cutting, Finishing & Pressing, Packing,
Delivered). Support filtering by status.


=====================================
11 — FINANCE
=====================================
Design the Finance screen, following the established style guide. It's a
financial overview for the business — accounts payable/receivable,
expenses, and margins.

Include KPI cards for Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, and Average
Margin, an expense list with an "Add Expense" action, and a way to drill
into a specific fiscal year's transactions (bank transactions, journal
entries, expenses) — design this as a "Browse by fiscal year" entry point
leading to a filtered transaction list grouped by month.


=====================================
12 — ACCOUNTING
=====================================
Design the Accounting screen, following the established style guide. This
is the formal bookkeeping view: balance sheet, accounts payable/receivable,
assets, equity, and expense categories (e.g. Depreciation, Equipment &
Machinery, Cash, Bank Account).

Design it as a structured financial report view — a Balance Sheet summary
(Assets / Liabilities / Equity) with expandable line items, and a way to
log a journal entry or bank transaction. This is a detail-heavy, precise
screen — favor clear tabular alignment and tabular numerals over decorative
cards.


=====================================
13 — BILLING
=====================================
Design the Billing screen, following the established style guide. It
handles client invoices and payments in multiple currencies (Cash/Bank,
with foreign currency conversion), referencing delivery challans.

Show an invoice list with client, amount, status (Accepted/Cancelled/
Collected), and challan reference. Include an invoice detail view with line
items and an "Add Payment" flow (amount, method: Cash/Bank/Credit, currency
conversion note). Add an action to generate/preview a PDF invoice.


=====================================
14 — BUDGET
=====================================
Design the Budget & Requirements screen, following the established style
guide. Staff submit budget/purchase requests (e.g. Equipment, Machinery,
Materials, Office Supplies) with a priority (Low/Medium/High) for approval.

Show a list of requests with category, priority pill, and status
(Pending/Approved), plus a submission form with category, amount, priority,
and additional context/notes field. Include an approval action for admins.


=====================================
15 — EMPLOYEES & HR
=====================================
Design the Employees & HR screen, following the established style guide. It
manages staff records and payroll.

Show an employee directory list (avatar, name, role, Active/Inactive
status) with search. Include an "Add/Edit Employee" form (name, role, bank
account details, status toggle), and a payroll section showing monthly
payroll records with an action to view/generate a salary slip (a clean,
official-looking payslip layout with earnings, deductions, and net pay).


=====================================
16 — DIRECTORS
=====================================
Design the Directors screen, following the established style guide. It's a
lightweight "about the company" and leadership directory page — Kazi
Manufacturing's Nepal and UK teams, factory-direct manufacturing model, and
office locations.

Show a company overview section (short "About Kazi Manufacturing" blurb,
factory-direct model, market/location info) followed by a directory of
directors/admins grouped by office (Nepal Team, Nepal Admins, UK/other),
each as a card with avatar, name, and role.


=====================================
17 — CUSTOMERS
=====================================
Design the Customers screen, following the established style guide. A
CRM-style contact list for clients (companies and individuals).

Show a searchable customer list with name, company, and location (e.g.
London), and an "Add/Edit customer" form. Include a customer detail view
showing contact info and linked orders/invoices if relevant. Support
swipe-to-delete or a clear delete action.


=====================================
18 — MARKETING
=====================================
Design the Marketing screen, following the established style guide. It
centers on a marketing content calendar for planning campaigns/posts.

Design a calendar view (month view with entries per day) plus a list view
toggle, and a way to add a new campaign/content entry with a date, title,
and notes. Keep it visually lighter than the operational screens — this is
a planning tool, not a data-dense one.


=====================================
19 — MESSENGER
=====================================
Design the Messenger screen, following the established style guide. It's a
simple internal chat/messaging tool between staff.

Design a two-pane-feeling mobile chat: a thread list (name, avatar, last
message preview, "Just now" timestamp) and a chat detail view with a
message bubble list and a send-message input at the bottom. Include a
pull-to-refresh "Refresh messages" affordance and an empty/"Thread not
found" state.


=====================================
20 — ADMIN PANEL
=====================================
Design the Admin Panel screen, following the established style guide. It's
a permissions/access-control console — admins configure which roles can see
which sections (Attendance, Billing, Employee and HR, Budget & Reqs, etc).

Design it as a settings-style list: each app section as a row with a toggle
or role-picker controlling visibility, grouped logically (Operations,
Finance, People, System). Keep the interaction pattern simple and
safe-feeling — this screen changes what other people can see.


=====================================
21 — BUG REPORT
=====================================
Design the Bug Report screen, following the established style guide. A
simple in-app form for staff to report issues.

Include a form with a title field, description, severity picker (Medium/
High/Critical), and a "Submit Bug Report" button, plus a confirmation state
after submission.


=====================================
22 — CHANGELOG
=====================================
Design the Changelog screen, following the established style guide. It
shows a chronological list of app updates, categorized by type (Feature,
Fix, Performance, Refactor, Chore, Docs, Style, Test, Build, Revert,
Update).

Design it as a timeline/feed: each entry with a date, a colored type tag,
and a short description. Group entries by release or month.
